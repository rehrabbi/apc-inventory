APC Inventory — Visual Design Directions

Your brand palette (APC red, warm black, cream) applied to a real inventory screen, three ways. The directions differ mainly in how loudly red is used — the core decision. Semantic colors (green/amber/red alerts) are shared and deliberately kept separate from the brand.

APC Red #D81F26 Warm Black #171210 Cream #F3EAE6 Warm Grey #8B8178
The red problem, solved: in inventory, red = danger (expired, critical). So brand red is used only for identity and key actions; routine alerts use green / amber, and red on data always means "needs attention." When red appears on a row, it means something.
Direction A

Controlled Signal

Recommended

Warm charcoal drives the interface; red is a controlled accent (logo, active tab, key highlights). Calmest and most readable — red on data always reads as an alert, never as decoration. Best fit for daily operational use.

Brand-Forward

Red leads — a bold red top bar and red primary buttons put the brand front and centre. Energetic and unmistakably APC. Alerts still use amber/green, and destructive actions become red-outline so they don't blend with red primaries.
